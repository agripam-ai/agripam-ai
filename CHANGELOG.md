## [1.0.0] - release pending

### Added

- Agricultural microbial-bank scoring, compatibility analysis and SynCom design.
- Evidence-aware chassis and genome-editing prioritization.
- Native and introduced-editor target assessment with explicit evidence labels.
- Parts-and-construct screening and experiment tracking.
- Reproducible example data, documented workflows and automated tests.
- Public Streamlit application and containerized local deployment.

### Scientific boundary

- Predictions support candidate prioritization; they do not demonstrate editing
  activity, strain-specific locus presence or experimental efficacy.
